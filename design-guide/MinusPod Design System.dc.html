A width class does not defend itself. A control sized with w-9 next to a long label is still a flex item with flex-shrink:1. Once the row cannot fit, the browser takes the width back: at 360px a switch beside a two-line label lost a third of its width. Give every fixed-size control in a flex row shrink-0.
This is why it kept coming back: the row only overflows at narrow widths, so the control looks right in desktop review and deforms on a phone. Check fixed-size controls at 360px, not just at the breakpoint you designed them in. Applies to the switch, icon buttons, badges, and the position column in a queue row.

Concise interface copy

Use short labels and actionable guidance in the app. Put detailed behavior and troubleshooting in user documentation. Keep units, consequences, recovery steps, and data-coverage warnings visible.

Aligned filters and date ranges

Filter groups use the shared filterGrid, filterLabel, and filterControl recipes from frontend/src/components/fieldStyles.ts. Labels sit above controls, columns have equal widths, and controls fill their columns with a 44px minimum height. Use min-width:0 on grid children so long selections stay inside the control. Preserve full-width credential and endpoint fields.

A date range is one nested two-column grid, including at 320px, 360px, and 390px viewport widths. From and To keep separate labels and values. Native date inputs fill the available width with min-width:0 and left-aligned values. Use the native picker affordance without an extra calendar button. Keep mobile input text at least 16px and preserve room for the full date value. Check empty and filled values in Chromium and WebKit, both light and dark, and preserve 44px touch targets.

Date fields

Every date input is the shared DateField component (frontend/src/components/DateField.tsx), never a bare <input type="date">. It carries the filterControl and inputBase classes plus the mobile overrides max-sm:px-0 max-sm:text-base, a left-aligned webkit date value and a min-w-0 datetime-edit, so the native iOS picker cannot widen the field past its grid cell. A range is two DateFields with cross min and max limits. The value commits on change and again on blur. Used by the Stats spend ledger and History > Downloads.

Tables & lists

Routine lists use a desktop table inside a card, thead bg-muted/50, uppercase 12px headers, rows divided by divide-border with hover:bg-muted/50. Below each table's measured content breakpoint the table is hidden and replaced by stacked cards, never a horizontally scrolled full table on phones. Low-priority columns drop at md/lg first. At every width where the table renders it fits its container with no sideways scroll; the overflow-x-auto wrapper is a safety net, not a layout. Column minimums add up. Cap long text with truncate max-w-[Npx] block and a visible or keyboard-accessible way to read the full value. Let an unbreakable value such as IPv6 wrap at its separators with a <wbr> after each colon; break-all splits IPv4 mid-octet. Give that cell a min-w floor so the column cannot collapse. If the columns still overrun, hide more of them at the next breakpoint or raise the card threshold (Processing History uses 1280px). Measure at 768, 1024 and 1280 px. Detailed comparison tables may scroll inside a named, keyboard-focusable region; routine lists must fit. The History page Downloads tab is the worked example: nine columns, cards below md, Podcast and Subscriber from lg, Location and Client from xl. A numeric column aligns its header the same way as its cells: pass align="right" to SortHeader and give the values tabular-nums. Text and date columns stay left.

Shared components

Three primitives carry most of the app’s interaction contracts. Every class string below is copied from the shipping component (Popover.tsx, usePopover.ts, SegmentedToggle.tsx, badgeStyles.ts). Import the component; never restate its recipe in a new screen.

Popover & usePopoverTabs

A panel portaled to document.body, placed against its anchor and dismissed with it. The hooks mount with the panel, so build the children behind the same open. style is the placement hook’s to write and is not a caller’s prop.

panel = fixed [left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2 when centered] max-w-[calc(100vw-2rem)] bg-card border border-border rounded-lg shadow-lg overflow-y-auto z-40 [&_:focus-visible]:ring-inset
  • Placement. align is left, right, or auto (flips to the edge that fits). The panel opens above its anchor when the space below cannot hold it, and caps its own height so it scrolls rather than overflowing. Fixed bars marked data-viewport-inset are subtracted from that room.
  • Dismissal. Escape, an outside click or tap, a focusin outside the anchor and panel (a modal or palette stealing focus), and the anchor scrolling out of view. Escape is caught at document and stopped there, so no window listener behind the panel also fires. Every close reports its reason: escape, outside, anchor-hidden.
  • Focus movement. The panel is portaled out of the tab order, so usePopoverTabs routes it: Arrow↓/Arrow↑ on the trigger opens and focuses the first/last control, Tab moves into the panel, Shift+Tab closes and lets focus carry on backward, Tab off the last control closes and continues past the trigger, and closeAndRefocus() returns focus to the trigger. Disabled and hidden controls are skipped.
  • Mobile placement. Below 640px the panel centers on screen under the trigger’s row: a toolbar that wraps on a phone leaves no side with room.
  • Layering. z-40 clears the z-30 sticky header and stays under the fixed z-50 status bar. Dialogs use z-60; popovers owned by a dialog use z-70. Focus rings inside are drawn inset, because the scroll box clips an outset ring.

Do / Don’t

The consistency rules that keep new features looking native.

Do
  • Import button recipes from buttonStyles.ts; add only layout at the call site. The base owns size, focus, and disabled state.
  • Pair iconTarget with iconVisual for icon buttons with a filled square; AuditionPlayButton is the reference.
  • Keep text labels on dialog footers with 3 or more actions at every width; below 640px they become equal-width columns (dialogFooterGrid).
  • Foldable phones: check 466 and 626px (portrait) and 890px (landscape), a folded and an unfolded foldable. The shell pads for env(safe-area-inset-left/right) via .safe-x, and dialogs stay in the right-hand segment under horizontal-viewport-segments: 2. The Feeds grid has two columns from 600px; stat and tile grids use even counts from 640 to 1023px.
  • Use token colors so every theme works; check dark + Obsidian before shipping.
  • Use cards below each table's measured content breakpoint. Processing History and episode costs use 1280px; Downloads uses 768px. Preserve sorting and actions in cards. Verify populated layouts at 320, 390, 466, 626, 768, 890, 1024, 1280, and 1440 CSS px.
  • Reuse shared components: Modal, DropdownMenu, Popover, SegmentedToggle, Checkbox, NumberInput, Pagination, SortHeader, CollapsibleSection, LoadingSpinner.
  • Give every interactive element transition-colors, disabled:opacity-50, and focus-visible:ring-2 ring-ring.
  • Make failure detail reachable without a pointer: a focusable disclosure that expands the text inline, with a copy action. A title= may repeat it, never carry it alone.
  • Give every standalone interactive element a minimum 44px box on coarse pointers at every viewport width: import touchTarget from buttonStyles.ts and put it on the button or link itself.
  • Add new statuses to episodeStatus.ts / segmentCategory.ts maps, not inline.
Don’t
  • Wrap recipes in a new <Button> component, or turn link-style actions and selected-state toggles (PageTabs, SegmentedToggle, pagination numbers) into button recipes.
  • Hardcode gray-* / white / hex colors: they bypass the theme palette.
  • Invent a bordered “outline” button; btnOutline is Secondary by design.
  • Add shadows to resting cards: shadow-lg/xl marks overlays only.
  • Mix badge shapes: it’s always px-2 py-0.5 text-xs rounded + 20% tint.
  • Use more than one Primary action per card/row; pair it with Secondary or Ghost.
  • Ship a desktop table without its mobile card variant.
  • Make routine lists depend on sideways scrolling, or fix them with break-all on addresses.
  • Give selects or textareas a different fill or radius. Use selectBase or textareaBase.
  • Enlarge a tap target with surrounding gap or padding on a wrapper. The clickable box is the element, not the space around it.
  • Leave a failure reason hover-only; touch and keyboard users never see a title.
  • Use rounded-full pills, gradients, or new fonts: Roboto only.
